BreezeSprint25 — Breeze-ASR-25 on Colab TPU

Transcribe Taiwanese Mandarin and Mandarin–English recordings into timestamped text.

Two cells: prepare, then upload. Completed recordings automatically download as timestamped TXT.

Experimental Breeze port: not yet physically TPU-tested by the packager.

This targets the ASR-25 Mandarin/code-switching checkpoint, not Taigi-specific ASR-26. Traditional Chinese is the model tendency, not a per-character guarantee.

Open in Colab · Source and validation

Independent integration. Application MIT; model weights Apache-2.0.
